How to build your first website

A plain, step-by-step path for a complete beginner: pick a name, get hosting, build three pages and publish.

BeginnerA few hoursNo coding needed

1

Decide what the site is for

Write one sentence: "This site helps [who] to [do what]." For example, "This site helps beginners start their first online business." A clear purpose makes every later choice easier, from the name to the pages.

2

Pick a domain name

The domain name is your web address, such as yourname.com. Choose one that is:

  • Short and easy to say out loud.
  • Easy to spell, without numbers or hyphens.
  • Related to your topic, or simply your own name.

Write down three options, because your first choice may already be taken.

3

Choose a hosting plan

Hosting is the computer that keeps your site online all day. For a first site, the cheapest plan that includes a domain and a free security certificate (the padlock in the browser) is enough. You can upgrade later when you have visitors.

Compare plans on three things: what the first term costs, what the renewal costs, and whether the plan includes a domain and email.

Check the renewal price before you pay. Many hosts offer a low first-term price and charge more when the plan renews.

4

Register your domain and set up the site

After you buy a plan, the host walks you through choosing your domain and creating the site. Most hosts offer two easy routes:

  • A website builder: drag and drop, and the quickest way to a finished page.
  • WordPress: more flexible and better for writing many articles, with a small learning curve.

If you plan to publish guides regularly, WordPress is a good long-term choice. If you only want a simple page quickly, start with the builder.

5

Build three pages

  • Home: who the site is for and what they will find.
  • About: who you are, and why you write about this topic.
  • Your first guide: one article that solves a single problem for your reader.

Use short paragraphs and clear headings. Write the way you would explain it to a friend.

6

Check it on your phone, then publish

Open the site on your phone before you share it. Make sure text is readable, buttons work and nothing is cut off. Then publish, and send the address to three people to ask what is unclear.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Spending weeks on the design before writing anything useful.
  • Buying the most expensive plan on day one.
  • Copying text from other sites. Write your own words.
  • Hiding that your links are affiliate links.
